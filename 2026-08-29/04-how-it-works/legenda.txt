How it works.

1. Restaurants join with a promo menu and a nearby school. School / PTA: $0. Enrollment is open through October 2.
2. Families dine November 2–15 — dine-in, takeout, or pickup.
3. 20% of each qualifying promo item goes restaurant → PTA within 15 days. We never hold the funds.

Drinks, tax, and tip stay with the restaurant.
