Restaurants. Families. PTAs.

PTA Restaurant Week is a social dining program that connects local restaurants, families, and school PTAs.

Inspired by NYC Restaurant Week, created in 1992.

Restaurants offer 2–5 promo items. Families dine in, takeout, or pickup. 20% of each qualifying promo item goes restaurant → PTA. School / PTA: $0. Always.
We never hold the funds.
