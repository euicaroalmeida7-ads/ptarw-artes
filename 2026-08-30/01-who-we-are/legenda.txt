This is PTA Restaurant Week.

PTA is the school's parent-teacher association: parents and teachers who support the local school.

PTA Restaurant Week is a two-week social dining program that puts that PTA at the same table as restaurants and families.

Inspired by NYC Restaurant Week, created in 1992.

Restaurants offer 2-5 promo items. Families dine in, takeout, or pickup. 20% of each qualifying promo item goes from the restaurant to the PTA. School / PTA: $0. Always.
We never hold the funds.
